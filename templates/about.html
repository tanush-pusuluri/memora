What is Memora?

Most AI tools do the thinking for you. Memora does the opposite — it uses AI to make you think. Every mode is built around writing things in your own words, because that's what actually makes information stick.

🃏 Flashcards

Enter a topic and get a set of Q&A flashcards. Try to answer each question before revealing the answer, then mark whether you got it or need more practice. You can customize the number of cards and difficulty.

📝 Practice Quiz

Answer multiple choice questions on your topic and get an instant score. Missed some? You'll get study tips and resource links for what you got wrong, and you can generate 10 more questions to keep practicing.

🧠 Brain Dump

The most powerful mode. First, read each concept's explanation and write it in your own words. Then, write it again from memory with no help. Research shows this two-step process is one of the best ways to make information stick.
